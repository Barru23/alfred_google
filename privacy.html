Alfred — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Who uses this app

Alfred is a personal, non-commercial assistant used exclusively by its developer, Gerard Barrufet Segarra, with his own Google account. It is not intended for, or made available to, any other user.

Google data accessed

  • Google Calendar (read-only): to read the owner's events.
  • Google Tasks (read-only): to read the owner's tasks.
  • Gmail (read-only): to read and summarise the owner's email.
  • Gmail (send): to send email only on the owner's explicit instruction.

How data is handled

Alfred runs on the owner's own computer. OAuth tokens are stored locally on that computer with restricted file permissions.

To prepare summaries (for example, a daily briefing), Alfred sends only the minimum data needed for each request to the AI model provider configured in Alfred (currently OpenAI): event titles and times, task titles and due dates, and the sender, subject and relevant excerpts of selected emails. This data is processed by that provider solely to generate the requested output for the owner. Model training on this content is disabled in the provider settings.

The resulting summary is delivered only to the owner, through the owner's own private channels (for example, the owner's Telegram account).

Email bodies and event or task contents are not stored in Alfred's logs or in any code repository. Google data is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with any other third party, and not used to train general-purpose AI or machine-learning models.

Alfred's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account page "Third-party apps & services" (myaccount.google.com/connections).
